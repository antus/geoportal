Utility networks, tunnels and geology are under the surface. In the 3D view the globe can become
translucent to show what is below, and the terrain can be excavated over a drawn area. 3D tiles
models can have their axes corrected and be clamped to the ground.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done before v1.2.0 (already in the product when the plan was written), wave 0.
  Translucent globe to see under the surface, terrain excavation over an area, axis correction,
  ground clamping and collisions for 3D tiles.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted for utilities, tunnels and geology; depends on what the
  Cesium view of GeoLibre exposes (globe translucency, clipping), to be verified before estimating.
  The legacy estimate was 0 days because the feature already existed; the estimate is the cost of
  rebuilding it.
