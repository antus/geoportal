A graduated map is only as honest as its classification. The style editor offers equal intervals,
quantiles, standard deviation, geometric intervals and natural breaks (Jenks), computes the breaks
on the values of the layer, saves them with the style and writes the method in the legend. Small
layers are classified in the browser; above a threshold the breaks and class counts come from the
data source (PostGIS), so that a layer of a million rows is not downloaded to be classified.
