---

Many point layers are read best as density. A heatmap style on point layers has a radius in ground
units (not screen pixels), an intensity, a weight taken from an attribute and a colour ramp; the top
of the ramp comes from a high percentile so that one hot spot does not flatten the rest. It works on
layers that load by area as the view moves.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. Heatmap as a companion layer fed by the geometries of
  the source layer, also for tiled WFS; radius in kilometres, ramp maximum from the 95th percentile
  of cells, weight from an attribute.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; on GeoLibre the heatmap is a native MapLibre or deck.gl
  layer, configured from the style grammar.
