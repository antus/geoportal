acceptance:
  - Selecting a vector layer opens the detail panel with its style section already open.
  - Selecting a remote service layer opens the panel on its general section, and no section that does not apply to that layer kind is listed.
  - Only one section is open at a time; opening another closes the previous one.
  - Asking for a section from elsewhere (for example from a "style" command) opens it and never closes it.
---

A fixed strip of five tabs, always present, makes the author hunt for the right one. The detail panel instead appears on the selection and shows exactly what that selection requires: the style of a vector, the appearance of a 3D mesh, the general settings of a remote service. Sections form a column with one open at a time, so a narrow panel never needs scrolling between two open sections. Which sections exist for each kind of object is decided in one place. Lazy sections (columns, popup, JSON, spatial filter) wake when opened.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. The tab strip became a column of sections with the relevant one open; opening and toggling a section became two separate functions after the style path test showed a request to open must never close.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — the principle applies to the layer and object panels of the map editor built on GeoLibre and to the Geoportal editors; check first what the GeoLibre panels already provide.
