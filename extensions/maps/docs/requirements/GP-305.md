id: GP-305
title: Highlight defaults are declared once in the theme and follow the basemap
area: maps
priority: P2
status: planned
depends: [MK-028]
estimate: [3, 5]
legacy: R-305
acceptance:
  - Switching from a light to a dark basemap changes the highlight colour of a selected feature.
  - The highlight colour reaches a WCAG contrast of at least 3:1 against the background of each shipped basemap.
  - Changing the theme value changes the highlight of an existing map whose layers were never customized.
---

Highlight colours should not be set layer by layer. Fill, outline, width, opacity and trigger (hover
or click) are declared once among the theme variables, can be overridden by an organization's theme
and, with a reason, on a single layer. The defaults are chosen for contrast against the active
basemap, so there are two pairs, light and dark, that switch with the basemap. A layer that never
touched its highlight follows the theme rather than copying its values.
