  - Between two updates a moving object's position is extrapolated from the time actually elapsed, without jumping back at the next update.
  - In the 3D view aircraft are drawn at their reported altitude with a drop line to the ground.
  - Labels and icons are oriented on the heading and scale with distance.

Position is estimated on the time actually elapsed, aircraft fly at their real altitude with a drop line, markers orient on the heading without going backwards, and they scale with distance. The legacy behaviour was built on the dropped map engine and has to be rebuilt on the GeoLibre real-time layer (deck.gl and Cesium).
