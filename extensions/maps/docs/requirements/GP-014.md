Readable labels need more than a font size. The label block of the style grammar names halo colour
and width, weight, italic, case transformation (upper, lower, capitalized, also after hyphens and
apostrophes), letter and line spacing, placement and offset, background with padding and a maximum
length with truncation. Each property is writable by hand in the style document and reachable from
QML and SLD import.
