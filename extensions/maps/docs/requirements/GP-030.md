title: Undo and redo scoped to the panel being edited
area: editing
priority: P2
status: proposed
estimate: [8, 12]
legacy: R-030
acceptance:
  - After three changes to a layer style, pressing undo three times restores the original rendering of the layer on the map.
  - Redo after undo reapplies the change, and a new change clears the redo history.
  - Undo in the style panel does not revert changes made in another panel or to another layer.
---

An author styling a layer or arranging a dashboard expects undo limited to the panel in use. It was removed from the old product and is also the prerequisite of an assistant that edits maps, dashboards and processes: its changes must be reversible step by step.
