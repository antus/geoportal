  - Two people editing different properties of the same layer at the same moment both find their changes after the session is saved.
  - Two people moving different vertices of the same feature at the same moment both keep their change.

Only if the relay model proves insufficient: synchronization by operational transformation or CRDT
with per-object granularity, so that simultaneous edits of the same map no longer lose one of the
two. It would most likely be built in or with GeoLibre rather than beside it.
