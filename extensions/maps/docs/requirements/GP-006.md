The layer list should say what each layer looks like without opening anything: a strip for a
graduated style, chips for a categorized one, a single sample for a simple style, and for a
map-server layer a scaled GetLegendGraphic image on a transparent background.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.2.1, wave 1. One miniature per layer card: the ramp of a graduated
  style, the colours of the categories and, for WMS, a small GetLegendGraphic.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; rebuilt in the layer panel of the maps extension.
- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: done. The rows of the published services of ext-catalog-geodata, the layer cards that exist
  today, show what each layer looks like (web/symbology.js, from sampleOf of web/style-themes.js): a strip
  with the colours of the classes of a graduated style in order, one chip per category colour up to eight
  and how many more, a sample of the symbol of a simple style, and for a layer the map server styles (a
  raster) the GetLegendGraphic of its WMS through the gate, on a transparent background; the detail of a
  layer shows the GetLegendGraphic in full, with the titles the SLD gives to the classes. Proved on
  GeoServer by tests/symbology.spec.ts and without a bench by tests/symbology.test.ts. The layer panel of
  the maps (GP-340) shows the same miniature and legend from the same modules.
