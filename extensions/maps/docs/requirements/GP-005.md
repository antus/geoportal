A designed look changes every screen, including those in sales material, at a low cost per day.
Geoportal ships a versioned file of curated colour ramps and categorical sets, each marked
colour-blind safe, used by the style editor and by the smart defaults. Map styles group a basemap, a
label style and a map accent, so one choice restyles the map coherently; the interface follows the
theme of the shell. An organization can override the palettes through its theme.
