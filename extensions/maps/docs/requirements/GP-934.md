A context is a snapshot of the view: layers, basemap, terrain, active map extensions, camera and linked dashboard. It restores the view even after layers were deleted and recreated, flies to its position, and editing it updates the saved state. Authors use contexts to tell a story or to switch between working setups on the same map.
