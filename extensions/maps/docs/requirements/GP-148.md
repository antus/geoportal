---
id: GP-148
title: Adding a control or widget to a map means switching it on, with a default position that never overlaps others
area: maps
priority: P1
status: proposed
estimate: [10, 15]
legacy: R-148
acceptance:
  - Switching on any combination of map controls and overlays leaves no pair of their rectangles intersecting, at four window widths.
  - Overlays anchored to the bottom start above any full-width band, and scale, credits and coordinates stay in the status band.
  - A corner chosen by the author wins, and the other overlays arrange around it.
  - Position and detailed options are behind a closed "Customize" section and are absent in view mode.
---

Adding a control forced the author to decide where it goes and how it behaves before knowing whether it was wanted. Each control, component or extension instead carries a default position and configuration: placing it on the map is switching it on, and details wait behind "Customize". Positions are declared as anchors and order, not coordinates: overlays sharing an anchor stack with a fixed gap, a bottom status band holds only scale, credits and coordinates, and full-width bands push bottom-anchored overlays up. Hand-written coordinates only worked for the combination that existed the day they were written.
