Drawing rectangles, polygons, circles and polylines; measuring distance, area, perimeter and bearing;
seventeen spatial tools (buffer, grids, Voronoi, triangulations, intersection, union,
simplification) on the geometries of the map. It is a baseline every map user expects. GeoLibre
already provides drawing and measuring; the work is to check what it covers, add the missing tools
through the Geoportal plugin, and keep drawn geometries usable as inputs of analyses.
