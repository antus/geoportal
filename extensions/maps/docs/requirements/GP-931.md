Eight live sources ready to use: flights and ships, earthquakes, fires, natural events, disaster alerts coloured by severity and about nine hundred public webcams. They are added with one click, without API keys. In the new architecture they are sources of the Geoportal real-time layer in GeoLibre and of dashboard widgets.
