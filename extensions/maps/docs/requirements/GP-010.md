  - A line width with stops 2 at zoom 10 and 8 at zoom 16 is drawn 5 pixels wide at zoom 13.
  - A layer with a visibility range 12–18 is not drawn at zoom 10 and is drawn at zoom 14.
  - The stops survive saving and reopening the map.
---

Any style property can take stops of the form zoom level → value: numbers are interpolated, colours
are blended, the curve can be smooth or stepped, and outside the stops the value is held rather than
extrapolated. Each layer also has a visibility range set with a slider.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. Any style property can have zoom stops (numbers
  interpolated, colours mixed, stepped or smooth, clamped outside the stops), stored in the grammar
  as a fourth kind of value; visibility range per layer.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; zoom stops map directly onto MapLibre zoom expressions,
  which should make the work cheaper than on the legacy engine.
