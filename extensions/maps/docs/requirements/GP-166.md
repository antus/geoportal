Control-room maps use motion as their main language: traffic flow, evacuation, the route of a rescue
vehicle, a power network. A rendering type for line layers and point series animates along the
geometry to show direction, speed and intensity, with density and colour driven by an attribute. The
panel shows only the controls that the chosen animation uses.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. Eight animated line materials of the legacy engine, each
  showing only its own controls, with density and colour driven by an attribute.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; on GeoLibre the animation becomes a deck.gl trips or
  animated-path layer, so the work is new rather than a port.
