  - The view moves to the extent of the new layer, and one undo restores the previous style and view.

A map that arrives already styled, labelled and with its legend is the difference between a product
that looks finished and an engine waiting to be configured. When a layer is added, Geoportal picks a
symbol from the geometry, a colour from the curated palette that differs from the layers already on
the map, a sensible text attribute for labels (mostly distinct, short values) and the extent known
from the catalog metadata or the capabilities. Label visibility depends on feature density, and
above a threshold labels stay off until asked for. Everything is reversible from the style panel and
from one undo action.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 1. Symbol from the geometry, a colour-blind-safe colour
  distinct from the layers already present, label field chosen by score, legend and zoom to extent,
  all undoable from the notification; label visibility distance stepped by feature count, labels
  opt-in above 20,000 features.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; the defaults become a pure function (geometry, feature
  count, attribute types, palette) in the maps extension, applied through the style translator of
  the Geoportal plugin in GeoLibre and reusable by the assistant.
