  - A layer whose legend switch is set to "only here" does not appear in the legend card of the same map opened by a reader.
  - When no layer is sent to the reader, the reader sees no legend card at all, and the author sees a message saying why.
  - The legend card title edited by the author is shown to the reader after saving; left at its default it follows the language of the reader.
  - A reader who switches a layer off from the legend card stops seeing it on the map, and the layer stays listed in grey.
---

A categorised theme with fifteen classes floods the reader's legend and hides the one line the reader was looking for. The author needs a switch per layer that says where its legend goes, "to the reader" or "only here", with symbols kept faded in the author's panel when it is off. Layers without a legend (3D tiles, basemaps) get no switch. The legend card title is editable in place, an empty legend says so instead of showing a mute frame, and the global legend switch only says whether the card starts open or collapsed. The legend card is also the reader's only command: the reader turns layers on and off from it, and layers the author excluded do not appear at all.
