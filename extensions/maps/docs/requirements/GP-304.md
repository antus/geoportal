acceptance:
  - Clicking a feature highlights it and clicking another removes the first highlight.
  - Closing the popup or panning the view keeps the feature highlighted until the selection changes or Esc is pressed.
  - A multiple selection highlights every selected feature, also in the 3D view.
---

Clicking a feature opens its popup, but the map must show which feature it is. One mechanism, based
on the selection model, works for every vector geometry and for the elements of a 3D model: the
selected geometry takes the highlight style and the previous one loses it; the highlight lasts as
long as the selection, not the hover, so it survives a view change and the closing of the popup; a
multiple selection highlights all its features; and in 3D the outline is drawn over the depth so
that a geometry draped on terrain is recognizable even at a low angle.
