  - On a touch device, pinch zooms, double tap zooms in and a two-finger drag tilts the 3D view.
  - Every map command has a touch target of at least 44 by 44 px below the medium breakpoint.
  - Feature information opens on a tap; no information is reachable only by hovering.
---

On tablets and phones the map must be usable with fingers: pinch, double tap and two-finger tilt on the 2D and 3D views, 44-pixel targets, panels that become bottom sheets, and selection that does not depend on hovering the pointer.
