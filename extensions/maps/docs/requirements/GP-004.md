Proportional symbols are a basic cartographic type. The size of points, icons and the width of lines
can be bound to a numeric attribute, with a linear or square-root curve (the latter keeps areas
proportional), a minimum and maximum size, and a legend that shows three reference values. Size
combines with graduated or categorized colour on the same layer.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 1. Size by numeric attribute for points, circles, icons and
  lines, linear or square-root curve; the legend shows the scale with three references; combines
  with graduated and categorized colour.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; size becomes a data-driven value of the style grammar,
  translated to MapLibre or deck.gl expressions.
