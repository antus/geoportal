  - A comment placed on a point of a map is shown at that place to every person who can see the map, and reopening the map shows it again.
  - A comment anchored to a feature of a layer stays attached to that feature and opens when the feature is selected.
  - Mentioning a person sends them a notification in their activity feed; resolving a thread hides it from the default view and records the resolver.
  - A person without read access to the map receives a refusal when listing its comments through the API.
---

People discuss a map where the problem is: a thread of comments anchored to the resource and
optionally to a coordinate or to a feature, with replies, mentions, resolution and an audit entry.
Comments are stored by Geoportal, and the GeoLibre map shows them as markers through the Geoportal
plugin. Notifications use the activity feed of Mosaikit, which also handles mail delivery
preferences.
