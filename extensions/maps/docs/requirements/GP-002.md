  - The legend card moved to a corner and the map saved, the map reopened in the viewer shows the card in the same place.

The legend is what the reader of a map sees, so it is derived from the style at every change instead
of being drawn by hand. Each layer has a caption and display names per class, each entry can be
switched off, and the legend sits on the map as a card that can be moved and closed, with its
position saved with the map. The legend button is available to readers of a saved map, not only to
its author.
