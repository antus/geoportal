A swipe between two basemaps or two rasters is not enough. Comparison must also divide two vector layers, label what each side shows (date, scenario, forecast against observed), and offer two synchronized side-by-side views, which read better than a swipe on a wide screen. One map-level control costs less than building comparison inside each of the four features that need it.
