The header of an open map answers three questions together: what am I looking at, who else is here, and what can I do with it. Presence avatars, sharing and the editor/view mode sit in one group on the right of the header, with the header component of mosaikit-ext (EXT-061) that dashboards and processes use, so that the three read the same. Tools such as place search and basemap stay in the map's tool bar.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. Presence, sharing and mode were grouped on the right of the resource header; search and basemap were left out on purpose, as a declared deviation from the original text.
- 13 October 2026: split — the header of Geoportal resources stays here; presence of people is MK-033 and the generic sharing model is an MK need. The legacy dependency on the navigation bar (GP-026) moved to Mosaikit with it.
- 14 October 2026: split — the header of dashboards and processes is EXT-061 in mosaikit-ext; the header of maps (`ext-maps`) stays here and uses the same component (ADR-0024).
