The popup is how most readers meet the data. The author picks a popup template (header, icon, body),
writes text in which the fields of the feature are interpolated, chooses whether it opens on click
or on hover, and whether it renders as a card on the map or as a side panel. Popups follow the
theme.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done before v1.2.0 (already in the product when the plan was written), wave 0.
  Six popup templates with header, icon and icon picker, fields interpolated in the text, on click
  or hover, as a card or a side panel, following the theme.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; popups of the Geoportal plugin in GeoLibre. The legacy
  estimate was 0 days because the feature already existed; the estimate is the cost of rebuilding
  it.
