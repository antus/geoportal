GeoFlow nodes call language models on the main providers to generate and extract, detect objects in
images, segment and upscale. AI credentials are encrypted and each organization has a budget with a
rate limit. The nodes are Geoportal's; the credential store and the per-organization AI budget, rate
limit and cost metering are generic and shared with the assistant of Mosaikit (MK-024), so they are
asked of Mosaikit.

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.2.0 or earlier, wave 0 (already in the product when the plan was born, tracked from 25 September 2026). No legacy priority.
- 13 October 2026: split — the nodes stay in the processes extension; encrypted credentials and the per-organization AI budget go to MK-062 and MK-111. Priority set to P2; estimate is for porting (legacy 0 days because already built).
