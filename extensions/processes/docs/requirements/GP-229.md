acceptance:
  - GeoFlow offers nodes to convert a model, extract properties, check information requirements, compare two revisions, compute a take-off and find interferences.
  - A process built with these nodes can be scheduled, and each run is recorded with its inputs, outputs and log.
  - A scheduled process that converts and checks every new model uploaded to a folder produces a revision and a check report for each.
---

Convert, extract properties, check information requirements, compare two revisions, compute a take-off,
find interferences: the same operations available as process nodes, so they become schedulable,
versioned and auditable like any other chain instead of buttons in the interface. It is also how an
organization automates the monthly intake of models from its designers.
