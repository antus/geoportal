Source nodes bring data into a process: queries on map features, weather, biodiversity, catalogs and
satellite imagery, Earth observation search and download, feature services and map servers,
uploaded files, SQL databases and remote processes, with ports generated from the service
description. They are what makes GeoFlow useful on real data rather than on samples.
