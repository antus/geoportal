Slope, aspect, hillshade, watersheds and flow accumulation, and visibility from a point on the globe.
The terrain model exists and the 3D view exists; the viewshed is the most spectacular demonstration
the product can make at almost no cost, and it recurs in archaeology, planning, telecommunications
and defence. Heavy raster work can later move to the GDAL sidecar of GeoLibre.
