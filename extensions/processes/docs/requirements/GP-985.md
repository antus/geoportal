  - The geospatial nodes (sources of map features and geodata, spatial operations, writing to PostGIS and publishing on GeoServer) are contributed to `ext-processes` by `ext-processes-geodata` (manifest id `dev.geoportal.ext.processes.geodata`) through the node contract (EXT-065).
  - With `ext-processes-geodata` not installed the palette of `ext-processes` has no geospatial node, and a process holding one opens with the placeholder of the platform (MK-135) on that node.
  - A process mixing generic and geospatial nodes runs on the executor of `ext-processes` and its run history shows both.
---

The process editor and its executor are common (`ext-processes` in mosaikit-ext). The nodes that know
geodata are Geoportal's: GP-924, GP-925, the analyses GP-160 to GP-163, GP-282, GP-283 and the other
geospatial nodes. They come in one extension, `ext-processes-geodata`, which requires `ext-processes`
and `ext-catalog-geodata`.
