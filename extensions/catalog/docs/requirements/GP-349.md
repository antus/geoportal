title: Publication to the public catalog and CSW can require a manager's approval
area: catalog
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [GP-246, GP-338]
estimate: [5, 8]
legacy: R-349
acceptance:
  - With review switched on for the organization, an owner's request to publish leaves the resource private and absent from the public catalog and CSW.
  - A manager approves the request and the resource appears in the public catalog and in CSW answers.
  - A manager sends the request back with a note, and the owner sees the note and the resource stays private.
---

Where an organization asks for it, data do not go out alone: the owner requests publication to the public catalog and CSW, a manager approves or sends it back with a note, and the data stay private until approved. It is the draft, approved, published path public bodies expect from a geoportal; a switch per organization turns it on.
