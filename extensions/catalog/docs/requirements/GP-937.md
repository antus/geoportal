  - Uploading a Shapefile, GeoPackage, GML, GPX, CSV (with separators or WKT), KML, KMZ or CZML produces a PostGIS table published as a GeoServer layer whose WFS answer contains the features.
  - Uploading a GeoTIFF produces a COG published by GeoServer whose WMS answer returns the image within its bounding box.
  - A file that cannot be converted is refused with the reason, and leaves no partial table or layer behind.

Shapefile, GeoPackage, GML, GPX, CSV with separators and WKT, KML and KMZ, CZML become a common vector
representation on the server; a GeoTIFF becomes an image with its bounding box. In the new architecture
there are no files on the application disk: vectors are loaded into PostGIS (GeoServer Importer or
direct writes) and rasters are stored as COG, both published in the workspace of the organization.

## History

  files kept on disk.
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — rebuilt as ingestion into PostGIS/GeoServer and COG; legacy estimate was

- 2026-10-13: in progress, phase F03 — the catalog loads uploads into PostGIS and Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF (ADR-0015).
- 2026-10-13: the catalog loads every upload in the request that stores it (ADR-0019: containers read
  by the catalog, geometries parsed by PostGIS, no GeoTools): vectors into the table of the technical
  name in the schema of the organization, defined as a layer that the alignment of GP-334 publishes;
  GeoTIFF into a COG made by the GDAL of PostGIS raster and stored with `FileStorage`; space reported

  vector formats through WFS on the GeoServer of the tests (`GEOPORTAL_TEST_GEOSERVER=1`); criterion 2
  waits for GP-334 to publish COG coverages. Still in progress.
- 2026-10-13: files are loaded by GDAL in a sidecar (ADR-0019): `distribution/server/loader` (official GDAL
  image pinned by digest, internal network only) streams the upload from a signed link, `ogr2ogr` into
  PostGIS or `gdal_translate` to COG; the catalog submits the job, the dataset is `loading` with its
  progress, and the outcome is taken over at the next reading (MK-126). Files with several layers ask
  which one to load. The readers of the catalog stay as the fallback, and for KML, KMZ and CZML. Tests
  tagged GP-937 against the real loader and PostGIS, a 200 MB file included. Still in progress
  (criterion 2: COG coverages of GP-334).
- 2026-10-13: the outcome of a job is taken over only in a request of the owner or of a person who
  edits the dataset (who stores the COG and keeps full access to it); a viewer sees it loading. A
  takeover in the background, without waiting for such a person, waits for MK-126.
- 2026-10-13: depends on MK-129 of Mosaikit, planned in F03 with the maintainer's approval.
- 2026-10-14: on Kubernetes the chart of Geoportal sets `geoportal.catalog.loader.url` and
  `geoportal.catalog.loader.source-url` through `mosaikit.extensions.dev.geoportal.catalog.settings`
  (MK-129), so the catalog of a cluster uses the loader of its release; the chart checks that both name

  tagged GP-937 in `distribution/cloud/test/chart-test.sh` (`helm template`), run by the CI on the

- 2026-10-14: done. Criterion 2 on the raster publication of GP-309: an uploaded GeoTIFF loaded as a
