  - Registering a STAC endpoint lists its collections with the period they cover, read by the server so catalogs without CORS headers work.

  - An Overture theme can be browsed and added to a map as a layer.

A STAC connection lists the collections of a catalog and serves the footprints of their scenes as GeoJSON; the server does the reading, because most STAC catalogs do not send CORS headers. On top of the connection, a browser filters by date and cloud cover and also covers Overture themes. A satellite scene is pixels until someone counts them, so its use is maps and processes, not dashboards.
