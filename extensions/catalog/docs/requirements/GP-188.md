---
id: GP-188
title: Vector tiles are generated by the engine that holds the data, with cache and generalization
area: data
priority: P1
status: proposed
estimate: [12, 18]
legacy: R-188
acceptance:
  - A layer bound to a connected PostGIS table of hundreds of millions of rows is drawn from vector tiles produced by the database (ST_AsMVT), and no request returns raw geometries.
  - A tile requested twice is served from the cache the second time, and invalidating the source makes the next request regenerate it.
  - At low zoom levels the tiles carry generalized geometries and stay below a declared size.
---

The engine that has the data writes the tile: the tiling function of PostGIS, its equivalent on warehouses, the embedded engine on columnar files. With a cache per tile, invalidation declared per source and generalization per zoom level. Tiling uploads (GP-058) covers data that entered Geoportal; a connected table of a billion rows has no other way to reach the map, and no amount of pushdown solves it, because the problem is drawing, not filtering. This is the key requirement of the large-data area.
