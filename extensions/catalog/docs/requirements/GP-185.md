acceptance:
  - A Zarr or NetCDF cube in storage or a registered bucket is listed with its variables, time range, vertical levels and extent.
  - A position, area, vertical profile and time-window extraction each return the values of the cube, matching those computed by a reference tool on the same file.
  - An external OGC API - EDR service can be registered and queried, and Geoportal publishes its own cubes through an OGC API - EDR endpoint that answers the standard queries.
---

Weather, climate, sea and modelled air quality live in cubes with time, height and band, not in tables or images. Geoportal reads Zarr and NetCDF where they are, with the extractions the trade uses: at a point, over an area, along a vertical, over a time window. It also speaks the standard of that extraction, OGC API - EDR, as a client of services that publish it and as a publisher of its own cubes. Without it the product misses the largest data source of environmental monitoring, and a forecast stays a curve per sensor instead of a field over a territory.
