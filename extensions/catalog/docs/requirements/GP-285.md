Eight hand-written public live feeds (earthquakes, natural events, disaster alerts, fires, flights, vessels, webcams) were moved into the curated catalog with a tag, keeping their rendering in a dictionary and failing a test when a tagged entry had no rendering.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done on 16 September 2026, wave 3; already superseded in part on 23 September 2026 by R-301, which turned the live feeds into map plugins because they are adapters of the product, not data.
- 13 October 2026: dropped — superseded by R-301 in the legacy itself; live feeds belong to the real-time layer of the GeoLibre plugin.
