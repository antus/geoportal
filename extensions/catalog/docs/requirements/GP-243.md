acceptance:
  - The public sheet of a dataset offers download in the supported formats, with the licence shown in the same box.
  - The sheet lists the WMS, WFS and other service addresses of the dataset on GeoServer, and they answer anonymously.
  - An anonymous download above the maximum size is refused, and requests above the rate limit get 429.
---

A download button in the formats the conversion chain produces, and next to it the service addresses of the dataset where it is published on GeoServer, with the licence in the same box: a downloadable format without a written licence is data nobody reuses. A rate limit and a maximum size per anonymous request are part of the item: it is the only place where a stranger can ask for an export.
