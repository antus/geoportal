The geospatial connectors of Geoportal, each an extension on the connection framework of mosaikit-ext (EXT-010): `ext-catalog-connection-ogc` (WMS, WMTS, WFS, WCS, processing services), `ext-catalog-connection-csw`, `ext-catalog-connection-arcgis` (MapServer and FeatureServer) and `ext-catalog-connection-tiles` (XYZ, TMS). The generic connectors (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle, WebSocket, SSE, MQTT, Kafka, REST) are EXT-040 to EXT-048 in mosaikit-ext; reading geometries from them belongs to `ext-catalog-geodata`. They are the ways geodata enters the catalog without being copied.

- 13 October 2026: geoportal — the connectors are rebuilt in the catalog backend; they are geospatial
  data sources, distinct from the file connectors of Mosaikit (MK-043). Legacy estimate was 0.
- 14 October 2026: split — the generic connectors (relational databases, streams, REST) are EXT-040 to EXT-048 in mosaikit-ext; the geospatial ones stay here as `ext-catalog-connection-<ogc|csw|arcgis|tiles>` (ADR-0024).
