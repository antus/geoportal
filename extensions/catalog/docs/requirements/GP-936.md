An uploaded file becomes a data resource of the organization: a readable title separate from the
technical name, a full lifecycle (create, rename, replace, delete), sharing with granular levels and
storage counted on the quota of the organization. On first start more than twenty sample datasets in
every format can be loaded, switchable off by configuration.

The generic parts (sharing model and subjects, storage quotas per organization) are Mosaikit's;
Geoportal applies sharing to geodata through GeoFence rules and counts the space taken in PostGIS and
by COG files.
