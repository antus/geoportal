  - The satellite and sensor sections list the curated open sources classified under them, even when their connector type is WMTS or REST.
  - Adding a curated entry creates a service or a connection in the organization according to its kind, and the entry then shows as already connected.
  - Every curated entry shipped with the product appears in at least one section.

Sections group sources by kind, but the curated catalog shipped with the product classifies open services by nature (satellite imagery, land cover, bathymetry, air quality sensors...) while connectors are typed by protocol: a satellite mosaic served as WMTS never reached the satellite section. A section therefore claims sources by connector type and by catalog category or tag, and the curated catalog offers the gesture that clones an entry into the organization. The catalog is extended with the open sources missing for the domains Geoportal serves, each checked before being listed.
