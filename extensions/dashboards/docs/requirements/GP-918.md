The map widget of `ext-dashboards-maps` (GP-984) takes part in the cross filters of the dashboards (EXT-074) through their filter contract: it receives attribute and time filters and applies them to its layers, and it emits the area in view or drawn as a spatial filter the other widgets apply. The map widget is the real viewer, GeoLibre embedded with `@geolibre/embed` and driven through the Geoportal plugin.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.2.0 or earlier, wave 0 (recorded for tracking on 25 September 2026).
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — to be rebuilt with GeoLibre as the map widget. Estimate set for the rebuild; the legacy 0 days only meant it already existed.
- 14 October 2026: split — attribute and time cross filters between widgets, and the filter contract, are EXT-074 in mosaikit-ext; the spatial part, with the map widget of `ext-dashboards-maps`, stays here (ADR-0024).
