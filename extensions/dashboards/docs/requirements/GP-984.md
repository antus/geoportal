  - With `ext-dashboards-maps` (manifest id `dev.geoportal.ext.dashboards.maps`) installed, the widget palette of `ext-dashboards` offers a map widget that shows a saved map of `ext-maps` or layers chosen from the catalog.
  - The map widget is contributed through the widget contract of `ext-dashboards` (EXT-065, MK-134), with no change to `ext-dashboards`.
  - A dashboard with a map widget opened in an installation without `ext-maps` or without `ext-dashboards-maps` shows the other widgets and the placeholder of the platform (MK-135) in the place of the map, naming what is missing.
  - Saving that dashboard keeps the definition of the map widget, which renders again once the extensions are installed.

Dashboards are common (`ext-dashboards` in mosaikit-ext); a map inside a dashboard is Geoportal's. The
map widget is its own small extension, `ext-dashboards-maps`, which requires `ext-dashboards` and
`ext-maps` (MK-132) and embeds the viewer of the map. It is also what takes part in the spatial cross
filters (GP-918). An installation that leaves out maps still opens every dashboard.
