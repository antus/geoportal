acceptance:
  - While editing a rule, the preview shows how many rows remain and the map already clipped as its subject would see it, before saving.
  - For a row hidden by a rule, the explanation names the rule, its author and the date it was written.
  - Saving or applying a rule writes one audit entry with the rule, the subject and how many rows it removes, not one entry per row.
---

A data rule written by hand can otherwise be checked only by asking a colleague to sign in. The rule
is composed with tools the product already has: the layer filter builder for the attribute part, an
area drawn on the map or taken from an existing geometry for the spatial part. Its effect is seen
before saving, through the eyes of the subject. The explanation answers why a row is not visible,
which rule removed it, who wrote it and when; applying a rule enters the audit as one fact.
