  - Publishing a dataset with a chosen layer name creates that layer in the GeoServer workspace of the organization, and a WMS GetCapabilities of the workspace lists it.
  - The publishing dialog shows the translated GeoServer style next to the Geoportal style before confirming.
  - After publishing, the layer appears as a service in the catalog with a health check status that turns to failed when GeoServer stops answering for it.
---

A dataset becomes a published service through one path: choose the destination, name the layer, check the translation of the style, publish, and see the result as a service in the catalog with its health check. In the new architecture the destination is the GeoServer workspace of the organization, loaded through the Importer or direct PostGIS writes, with raster as COG; the published layer is protected by the GeoFence rules Geoportal generates. It is the promise that the product enters an existing infrastructure instead of asking to replace it.
