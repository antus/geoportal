area: catalog
priority: P1
status: in-progress
depends: [GP-983, EXT-018, EXT-019, EXT-026, EXT-040]
decisions: [ADR-0024, ADR-0028, ADR-0030]
estimate: [3, 5]
acceptance:
  - A table or view with a geometry column of a PostgreSQL connection, found by the inspection of the connection (EXT-018), offers the use "map", contributed by `ext-catalog-geodata` through the uses of a piece (EXT-019), next to the uses of the other extensions.
  - Choosing it creates a layer reached through the gateway with the data rules applied, without copying the table, and the layer keeps a link to its connection and table.
  - Without `ext-catalog-geodata` the connection and its tables work as plain connections and the use "map" is not offered.
---

Split from GP-983: the file manager came first, and the geospatial use of a database connection
comes in the phase where connections are built, on the PostgreSQL connector of mosaikit-ext
(EXT-040), the inspection of a connection (EXT-018) and the uses that other extensions contribute to
a piece of a connection (EXT-020).
