  - A write to the source data of a layer by a person with the editor profile is refused by the API and by GeoServer (WFS-T answers with an access error).
  - A person with the surveyor profile can commit edits to a layer assigned to them and gets a refusal on any other layer of the same organization.
  - Removing a layer assignment takes effect on the next request without signing out.
---

Roles should name a job rather than a level of permission. Two jobs exist and had no home: an
editor who publishes maps and dashboards without touching source data, and a surveyor who edits only
the layers assigned to them. In the new product they are profiles declared by the Geoportal
extensions on the generic profile model of Mosaikit; what is specific to Geoportal is the scope
"assigned layers" and the mapping of these profiles to GeoFence rules so GeoServer applies the same
limits as the API.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done on 14 September 2026, wave 3 (not released). Roles became data with a
  scope (own, organization, assigned, system) and a single permission resolver able to explain its
  answer; on 10 October 2026 editor and surveyor were redefined as profiles (R-333).
- 13 October 2026: split — the generic profile and permission model goes to Mosaikit
  (MK-056); Geoportal keeps the two profiles and the assigned-layer scope. Estimate reduced
  from 15–22 days because the resolver is no longer Geoportal's.
- 2026-10-14: split with the maintainer's approval: "the editor changes and publishes maps and
  dashboards" moved to GP-976 (F09), which needs those extensions; editing limits stay here, in M1.
- 2026-10-14: in progress — editing limited to assigned layers (WFS-T rules in GeoFence), on top of GP-335.
- 2026-10-14: done — the catalog declares `edit-layer-data`; the profiles Editor (without it) and
  Rilevatore (with it) are made by the organization; layers are assigned to surveyors in the details of
  a publication. WFS-T through the catalog and through GeoServer (GeoFence rules per assigned surveyor)
  needs the permission and the assignment; a removal rewrites the rules at once (ADR-0023). Tests
  `SurveyorRulesTest`, `WfsTransactionTest`, `SurveyorEditingTest` (GeoServer) and `tests/editing.spec.ts`.
- 14 October 2026: belongs to `ext-catalog-geodata`, the geospatial part of the catalog (GP-983); Geoportal composes mosaikit-ext (ADR-0024).
- 2026-10-14: issue #38, a surveyor also limited by data rules on a layer could not edit it. Decided with
  evidence (ADR-0025): GeoFence gives a transaction the area of a `LIMIT` and the `cqlFilterWrite` of the
  `ALLOW`, and GeoServer refuses inserts outside them and limits updates and deletes to the features inside.
  Such a surveyor now writes within the area and the row filter through the WFS service; hidden columns stop
  writes; the catalog API answers `data-rules-service` or `data-rules` with the reason. Tests
  `SurveyorRulesTest`, `SurveyorEditingTest` (GeoServer, tags GP-036 and GP-308) and `tests/editing.spec.ts`.
