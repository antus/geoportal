  - Datasets chosen by their owner are published, with their metadata, to an existing GeoNode and appear in its catalog.
  - Harvesting a GeoNode catalog lists its datasets in the Geoportal catalog as external resources.
  - Permissions in GeoNode never change what a person may see in Geoportal.

Only for organizations that ask for it or already run GeoNode: Geoportal publishes data and metadata
to an existing GeoNode, or harvests its catalog. GeoNode never decides Geoportal's permissions. It is
outside the planned total until someone asks for it.
