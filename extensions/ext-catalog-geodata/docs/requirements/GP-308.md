  - A hidden column is missing from WFS and GetFeatureInfo answers for the subject of the rule.
  - When two rules apply to the same subject and layer, the answer contains only what both allow.

Permissions stop at the resource (this map yes, this layer no), while real work asks for finer
limits: an office sees only the records of its municipality, a district technician only the sensors
of the district, a consultant everything except the personal data. A data rule is one object: a
subject (role, profile, group or person), a dataset (source, layer or table) and three limits: an
attribute filter, an area and the readable columns. Data rules are distinct from permissions. The
resolver answers the questions a badly written rule leaves open: rules that apply together
intersect, a group membership and a personal rule compose the same way, and with no rule the
permission alone decides. Rules are translated into GeoFence rules by Geoportal.
