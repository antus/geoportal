area: data

acceptance:
  - A dataset of polygons is labelled with the polygon sign and a count such as "19 polygons" in the Data list, its detail and the "Add layer" panel.
  - A dataset mixing points and lines shows both signs.
  - A raster or a dataset with no geometry shows no geometry sign, and a dataset not yet inspected is distinguishable from one inspected and found empty.
---

People open a file only to learn whether it holds points, lines or polygons, and the symbology they pick next depends on it. The geometry type is read once where the format declares it and scanned where it does not, stored with the dataset and shown as a drawn sign (dot, polyline, filled shape) next to the count: in the Data list and card, in the detail and in the "Add layer" panel. With data loaded into PostGIS, the type of a loaded layer comes from the geometry column; scanning remains for files read in place. It is the first slice of the upload report (GP-057).
