The search found maps, dashboards, processes and uploaded files, but not connections, OGC services or curated catalog entries: half of the Data page could not be found by name. Geoportal's catalog extension contributes a search provider for these sources, and each result leads to the right section, not just to the page. The search box, pills and provider mechanism are Mosaikit's.
