The dashboards extension offers the widget catalog of the legacy product: thirty-one basic widgets (charts, indicators, tables, content and maps) and twelve operations-room widgets (indices, linear and radial indicators, clock, weather, news ticker, alert feed and the smart grid). It is the base every dashboard is built from.
