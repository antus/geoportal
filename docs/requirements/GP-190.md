---
id: GP-190
title: Approximate answers always declare that they are estimates and on which sample
area: data
priority: P2
status: proposed
estimate: [6, 9]
legacy: R-190
acceptance:
  - A dashboard figure computed on a sample shows that it is an estimate and the size of the sample, for example "about 4.2 million, on a 1% sample".
  - An approximate distinct count is labelled as approximate, and an exact count is not.
  - A progressively refined chart or map marks itself as partial until the full answer has arrived.
---

Sampling at the source, approximate distinct counts and rendering that refines as data arrives make large sources usable. The one constraint that makes approximation acceptable belongs in the interface: the figure always says it is an estimate and on which sample. A product that shows a wrong exact number loses at the first check; one that shows "about 4.2 million, on a 1% sample" has nothing to hide. The same rule as the provenance of every figure applies.
