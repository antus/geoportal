  - A report set to mail delivery arrives as an attachment to the listed addresses after the execution.
  - A report set to bucket delivery appears as an object in the configured bucket.
  - A report published with a 30-day retention is no longer reachable by its link after 30 days.

Delivery is part of the report template: the generated PDF is attached to an email to chosen people,
placed in a storage bucket, or published as a resource with a link and its own retention period. The
mail service, the storage, retention and links are Mosaikit capabilities that Geoportal uses.
