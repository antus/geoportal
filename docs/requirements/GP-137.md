Sample content is the shop window: it should look like the best work of the product, not its first
draft. Every demonstration map and dashboard uses the cartography of this area where its data fits:
density, hexagons, animated flows, hatching, zoom-dependent styling, composed ramps, label
typography.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. Eight demonstration maps each took a wave-2 rendering
  chosen on its data (heatmap, hexagons, animated flow, hatching, zoom interpolation, composed
  ramps).
- 13 October 2026: geoportal — still wanted; the sample content must be rebuilt on the new
  extensions anyway, and should show the renderings of GP-010 to GP-232.
