OGC SensorThings is the standard in which a network arrives with identity, location, units and history together (Things, Locations, Datastreams, Observations). Inbound, it is the connector that fills the device registry instead of a form per sensor; outbound, it publishes the organization's networks, so the product becomes a source for others. Public tenders name it, and it is the way to take over an existing network in an afternoon.
