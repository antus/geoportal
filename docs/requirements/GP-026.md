Every page has the same top bar: destinations, a search that finds everything, the notification bell, the active organization always visible and switchable, and the account menu with preferences (theme, language, tour) also available to anonymous visitors. It collapses in declared steps as the window narrows, and on a phone the destinations move to a bottom bar.
