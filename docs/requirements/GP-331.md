In the server edition every account, local ones included, lives in the Keycloak realm of its
organization, with passwords, second factor and lockouts handled by Keycloak and the organization's
single sign-on federated from there. One emergency administrator account stays outside Keycloak:
separate address, internal network only, mandatory second factor, every use audited and notified to
the other administrators, switched on and off only by server configuration. The portable edition
keeps accounts in the product.
