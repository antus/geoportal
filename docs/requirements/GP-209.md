  - In walk mode the camera stops at walls and slabs of the model instead of passing through them.
  - Gravity keeps the eye at the declared height above the floor, and stairs or a storey switch move the walker between storeys.
  - Leaving walk mode returns the camera to where it was before entering.
---

A first-person walk that knows the model is there: collision, gravity, declared eye height and speed,
moving from one storey to another, and an exit that brings the camera back where it was. The old walk tool
was made for terrain and went through walls. It is also the gesture that sells a model in a demonstration
more than any table.
