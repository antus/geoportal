area: monitoring

legacy: R-086
acceptance:
  - A person who takes an alert becomes its owner, and the alert shows as acknowledged to every member who sees it.
  - A snoozed alert sends no notification for the chosen time and notifies again afterwards if still active.
  - An alert not acknowledged within the rule's escalation time is delivered to the on-call recipient of the rule.
  - Alarm, acknowledgement and escalation appear in the audit with who and when.
---

An alert has an owner: someone takes it, silences it for an hour, or it goes up to the on-call person after a given time. In an operations room, who knew what and when is the whole point, so these transitions are audit entries.
