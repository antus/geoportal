acceptance:
  - An information requirements file (for example IDS) can be attached to a model or to a project.
  - The check lists, for each element, the required properties that are missing or have invalid values.
  - The report can be downloaded, and the revision shows a pass, warning or fail status.
---

The information requirements of a contract as a file, the per-element check of what the model should
carry and does not, the downloadable report and a traffic light on the revision. It is the difference
between "the model can be seen" and "the model can be delivered", which is the question of whoever
receives a BIM model under contract.
