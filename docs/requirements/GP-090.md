acceptance:
  - After the hot retention period, raw observations are no longer in PostgreSQL and are readable from the object store archive.
  - A time chart over a month of a high-frequency sensor is served from the resampled series and shows the full period.
  - Alerts are not resampled or deleted by the telemetry retention.
---

High-frequency measurements need a retention path that does not fill the operational database and does not lose history: a resampled series for maps and charts, raw data in cold storage on the object store. Telemetry is resampled, alerts are not: an alert is audit material, so they have two policies and two tables.
