acceptance:
  - With the organization's public switch off, every public catalog page, map preview and anonymous OWS request of that organization is refused.
  - A new resource is not public until someone makes it public; GeoServer refuses anonymous access to its layer until then.
  - The administrator sees the list of everything currently public, with who made it public and when.
  - Anonymous accesses to public resources appear in the audit.
---

A switch per organization and one per resource, refusal by default, the list of everything exposed to an anonymous visitor right now with who exposed it and when, and anonymous accesses in the audit like any other access. It comes **before** anything that exposes data: a public view built before the switch that decides what is public is born with an implicit rule. What is public is one queryable list, not a property scattered over tables, and it drives the GeoFence rules for anonymous access to GeoServer. The organization switch turns the whole public surface off at once.
