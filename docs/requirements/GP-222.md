acceptance:
  - Converting a model with spaces creates a polygon layer in PostGIS with one feature per space, with area, storey and use.
  - The layer is published by GeoServer and its WFS GetFeature returns the spaces at their georeferenced position.
  - The layer can be styled, filtered and used in a dashboard like any other polygon layer.
---

The rooms of a model extracted as georeferenced polygons with area, storey and use. It is the cheapest
and most profitable bridge of the area: once a room is a polygon it enters everything the product
already does (thematic styling, tables, dashboards, joins, alert rules) without anything 3D needing to
know about it.
