  - The run log shows the external job id, its log excerpt, its outcome and its reported cost.
  - A server restart while the external job runs resumes polling and completes the run.

A node submits the work to the customer's engine (Spark, a warehouse job, a managed stream service)
and waits for the outcome, with parameters, log and cost reported in the run. It is the same shape the
product already has for WPS and OGC API Processes (external job, polling, resume at the end) with
one more dispatcher. It is the coherent answer to the decision not to ship a distributed engine: when
volume exceeds what the process can do in-house, delegate to whoever owns the cluster.
