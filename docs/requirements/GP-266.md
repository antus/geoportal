The audit log keeps who, when, what and from where, with a declared retention that deletes by itself above a system minimum, a hash chain that proves no entry was changed, an output as one JSON line per entry towards the log collector of the organization (file, syslog, standard output), and a catalog of events that must leave a trace. Geoportal extensions write their events (data access, sharing, GeoFence rule changes, exports) through it.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 3. Closed on 25 September 2026 with one hash chain per organization plus one for the system, writes in their own transaction, anchors at retention and restore, a JSON output and a catalog of 36 mandatory events.
- 13 October 2026: moved — the audit log is a kernel service for every extension; Mosaikit records audit for files (MK-041, MK-044) but has no tamper-evident log with retention and export yet.
