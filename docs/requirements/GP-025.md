The interface uses a small motion scale (fast, normal, slow and one curve) instead of dozens of copied durations; menus and dialogs open with the same short transition as other panels; list pages that wait more than a second show skeletons shaped like the real cards; numbers ease only when they change under the eyes. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. "Nothing here" and "not arrived yet" are different states.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. Measured first (1.6 to 3.1 s of spinners, 32 distinct durations), then a motion scale, skeletons on four list pages and eased counters; no animation library was needed.
- 13 October 2026: moved — motion tokens and skeleton components come from the Fluent UI kit wrapped by Mosaikit (MK-026); Geoportal list pages use them.
