  - A new organization finds templates for accessibility statement, privacy notice, terms of use and data licence, legal notes and cookies, with the fields to fill highlighted.
  - A template with unfilled fields cannot be published.
  - An automated accessibility check of the public pages (contrast, reading order, text alternatives, labelled forms) reports no violation at the declared level.

Accessibility statement, privacy notice, terms of use and data licence, legal notes, cookies: not empty pages to fill but templates with the organization's fields highlighted, because a compliance page nobody fills is worse than none. It includes the measurement the accessibility statement presupposes, on the public pages only (catalog, data sheet, map preview), before signing a document that declares the conformance level.
