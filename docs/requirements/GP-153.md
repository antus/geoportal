Sources are grouped by what they are, not by the protocol used to reach them: satellite data, sensors, catalogs, real-time services, databases, map services (standard and not), files, and reports. People look for what they have, and every new kind of source already has a section to land in. The reports section is the declared home of report templates, the composer and scheduled deliveries. Each count distinguishes "none", "some" and "could not count". From each source one can later see what derives from it (the lineage requirement).
