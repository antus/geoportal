One path replaces the many "New" buttons spread over separate views. Step 1, **what it is**: the
connector types with their logos, grouped by the categories of the catalog, plus a file and the curated
catalog entries. Step 2, **how to connect**: the connection form of that type, where the connection test
is a preview of the content rather than a green tick. Step 3, **how it is named and found**: name,
description, thumbnail (generated from the name until saved, or uploaded), the fixed type and free tags.
The group in which the type was chosen becomes the category.
