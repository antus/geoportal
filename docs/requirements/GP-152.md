The list pages of maps and datasets take the shape of the list pages of the common extensions (EXT-062): filters in a side column, a fixed header with counts, and a selection model for favourites, tags, publishing and deletion.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. One shared list module for six lists, side filters, fixed header, a single 296 px card and multi-selection with a bottom bar.
- 13 October 2026: split — the list pattern (facets, fixed header, selection bar) is generic UI kit material; the Geoportal lists, their facets and bulk actions stay here.
- 14 October 2026: split — the list pages of dashboards, processes, files and connections are EXT-062 in mosaikit-ext; those of maps and datasets stay here (ADR-0024).
