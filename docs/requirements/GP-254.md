For each installed application: version, author, origin, state of its service, granted scopes with the date of consent, last use. Actions: turn on and off for the organization without uninstalling, decide who uses it (roles, groups or people, denied by default), revoke a granted scope, open the audit filtered on the application, and an emergency switch that stops it for everyone with the reason shown.
