  - The lineage panel of a source lists the layers, maps and dashboards that depend on it, so that the impact of changing it is visible before changing it.

The product has all the steps of the chain (source, ingestion, repeated analysis, threshold, alert, map, dashboard, report) but not the thread that ties them: whoever reaches a layer does not know where it comes from, and whoever changes a source does not know what will break. From any artifact one goes up and down the chain in one panel with the same shape everywhere and a clickable graph. It is a recurring audit request and the first question of every acceptance test.
