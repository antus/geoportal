acceptance:
  - Dragging a field of the layer into the builder adds it to the form, and the editing panel shows it at that position.
  - A field made conditional appears nested under the field it depends on in the builder, and the edit form shows it only when the condition holds.
  - Saving the form and reopening the layer in another map shows the same form.
---

Drag fields from the layer or add new ones, group them into sections, make them required, and nest
conditional fields visibly instead of hiding the rule in a property sheet.
