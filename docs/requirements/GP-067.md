acceptance:
  - Each analysis run from the map appears in the map history with its tool, parameters and outcome, whether it ran in the browser or on the server.
  - Re-running a history entry in one click produces the same result layer; changing a parameter and re-running produces a new one.
  - A failed entry shows the reason of the failure and the input that caused it.
  - The history is visible to people who can edit the map and not to people who can only view it.
---

Every execution started from the map is recorded on the map as a reproducible step, whether it ran
in the browser or on the server: re-run in one click, change the parameters and re-run, and read why
a run failed. The history records the step, not the engine. It is saved with the map, per person,
kept as long as the audit, and visible to those who can edit that map.
