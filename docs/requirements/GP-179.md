title: Quality checks mark each observation as good or suspect before rules read it
area: monitoring

status: proposed
depends: [GP-178]
estimate: [6, 9]
legacy: R-179
acceptance:
  - A reading outside the declared range is marked suspect and raises no threshold alert.
  - A value repeated unchanged beyond the frozen-value window marks the following readings suspect and raises one device alert.
  - Suspect readings are shown as such on charts and are excluded from rule evaluation.
---

Out of range, frozen value, impossible spike, slow drift: the checks that decide whether a reading is reliable, marked on the observation rather than inferred downstream. They come before alerts: a broken sensor sending -999 for six hours otherwise produces a flood of real alarms on false data, and a rules engine that cries wolf loses credit in a week. Rules only read good observations; suspect ones raise a device alert, which goes to a different recipient.
