Ten administration screens (administration, organizations, platform and organization users, security, audit, metrics, configuration, billing, settings) had ten different shapes, with no common place for search, filters and row actions. One model: metrics on top, search on the left, filters above the table, row actions on hover, detail in the context pane instead of a dialog. Migrating all the screens is half the cost and the whole value.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: to do, wave 3. From the UX redesign proposal: ten administration pages with ten shapes.
- 13 October 2026: moved — these administration screens are the kernel's (organizations, people, security, audit) and the pattern is generic; Geoportal administration sections, if any, follow it.
