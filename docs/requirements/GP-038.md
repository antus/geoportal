  - Opening a valid share link of a map without an account shows the map with its layers, and GeoServer serves those layers through the gateway for that link only.
  - After the link is revoked or expires, the same tile and feature requests through the gateway are refused.
  - The share dialog of a map offers an embed snippet, a QR code and a preview image, and the embedded map loads in a page of another site.

A link with a role (viewer, commenter, editor), an expiry and optionally a password, revocable in
one click and audited at every use, is generic sharing and comes from Mosaikit. Geoportal adds what
makes it work for geodata: the link must open the map or dashboard together with the layers it uses,
which means the gateway and the GeoFence rules accept the link as a subject; and the share dialog
offers an embed snippet, a QR code generated locally and a preview image of the map.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 1. Links with role, expiry, bcrypt password and use count;
  revocation kept the history; QR generated in house. Known limit: layer files stayed readable to
  anyone knowing their id.
- 13 October 2026: split — the link subject, roles, expiry, password, revocation and audit are
  generic (MK-055); Geoportal keeps link access to layers through gateway and GeoFence, and
  the embed and preview. Estimate reduced from 10–15 days for that reason.
