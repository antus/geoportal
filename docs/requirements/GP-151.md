Limits apply only where plans are in use, with two distinct switches: enforcing limits and selling.
Some installations want seats counted by contract without selling anything. Turning enforcement on
in a grown installation first reports what would exceed the limits, and limits then apply to new
actions only, never retroactively. The state is visible in administration and every change is an
audit entry.
