  - Each network shows the share of reporting devices, expected versus received completeness, median delivery delay and silent devices with their silence time.
  - The indicator has a history shown in a dashboard widget.
  - A rule on the network's reporting share raises an alert when it drops under its threshold.
---

Not the health of a single device, but of the network as a whole: share of devices reporting, expected versus received completeness, median delivery delay, how many are silent and for how long. One indicator per network with its history and threshold, watchable by rules like any other measure and shown in a dashboard. A monitoring service is measured on data availability, and this is what operators look at every morning.
