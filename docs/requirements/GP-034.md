A single email field: home realm discovery sends the domain of the address to the identity provider
of its organization without revealing which organizations exist, and the address travels as a login
hint so it is typed once. After authentication a person with several organizations chooses the
workspace, with the last one used first; with one workspace nothing is asked.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 1. Discovery by email with a uniform answer, login_hint to
  the realm, and a workspace choice after authentication remembered server side across devices.
- 13 October 2026: moved — choosing the organization is MK-017 (membership of several organizations);
  discovery of the realm from the email domain is generic and Mosaikit lacks it, hence the MK need.
