  - With the public-administration presentation turned on for an organization, its public catalog and public map pages show the institutional header with the name and contacts of the body, the footer with the mandatory links, and breadcrumbs.
  - The same pages of an organization that has not turned it on show none of these blocks.
  - The typography, colour scale and components of those pages use the values of the public-administration theme.
---

The design guidelines for public digital services apply to services of a public body, and a product that hosts such a service must be able to follow them without losing its own identity: an alternative presentation, not a rewrite. The theme itself (typography, colours, components) is available as a Mosaikit theme extension; Geoportal adds to its public pages the blocks an institutional service must have (header with the body and its contacts, footer with the mandatory links, breadcrumbs), turned on per organization.
