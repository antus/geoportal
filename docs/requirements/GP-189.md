title: A hierarchical grid index (such as H3) serves as a spatial key for aggregation and joins
area: data

legacy: R-189
acceptance:
  - A source can be given a derived grid cell column at several resolutions, computed from its geometry.
  - A hexagon aggregation layer requests cells at the resolution matching the zoom and shows counts equal to those of a direct spatial query.
  - A spatial join between two sources on an engine without spatial functions runs as an equality on the cell key and returns the expected pairs.
---

A column derived from the geometry on a hierarchical grid, at several resolutions. It enables two things: aggregating by cell at the resolution the zoom asks for (the surface the hexagon rendering needs) and expressing a spatial join as an equality of keys instead of a geometric predicate, which is how a spatial join runs on an engine that knows nothing about geometry. On warehouses where geometric functions are missing or cost a full scan, it is the only way.
