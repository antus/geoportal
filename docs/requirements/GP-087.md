  - An active alert appears on the map as a marker coloured by its severity, and disappears when the alert returns to normal.
  - The area of an area-based rule is drawn on the map while its alert is active.
  - Clicking an alert marker zooms to the station or geometry that raised it and opens its details.
---

Active alerts appear on the map as pins with their severity, with the area of the rule drawn, and one click leads to the station or geometry that raised them. In the new architecture this is a reserved alert layer driven by the Geoportal plugin inside GeoLibre.
