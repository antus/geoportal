Reports, official acts, scanned maps: files that are not geographic data but stand next to them. The
catalog keeps them as resources of its own kind, stored in the file service of Mosaikit and not in
GeoServer, registered so that they are found, shared with the same subjects and levels as datasets,
and linked to the data they refer to (ADR-0014). Their RNDT metadata come with GP-338 and their public
sheet with GP-241.
