acceptance:
  - Clicking a card opens the detail panel on the right edge, over the list, without moving the other cards.
  - The facts and tags of the source stay in place while the list of its parts (for example forty WMS layers) scrolls; the actions stay at the bottom.
  - Clicking the same card again or clicking outside the panel closes it; clicking another card shows that card's detail.
  - The open card is visibly marked as selected and announced with aria-current.
---

The detail of a source overlays the list instead of sitting beside it, so looking at one source does not
reflow all the others. It uses the language of the side panels of the map tools: attached to the right
edge, full height, header, body, fixed footer. Only the list of parts scrolls, so with many layers one
still sees which source they belong to. The actions (create a map, dashboard or process; edit, test,
delete) stay at the bottom, since they come after reading. The whole card opens the detail, so a
truncated description needs no popup of its own. The person's own sources come before curated catalog
entries in every sort order.
