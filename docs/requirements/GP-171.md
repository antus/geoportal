When a layer carries a value produced by a model (probability of a leak, risk, suitability), the
popup of the feature shows the factors that explain it with weight and direction, for example pipe
age, material, pressure, maintenance history. Below, the model, its version and its last training
date from the model registry. A score without its factors is a number a technician cannot contest,
and therefore does not use.
