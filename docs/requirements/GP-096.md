  - The transcript of the assistant's actions for an organization is visible to its administrators, with prompts, tool calls, generated SQL and outcome.

  - The context sent to the model by the Geoportal tools contains layer names, field names and types and the current view, and no geometry or attribute values.

Transcript per organization of the assistant's actions, cost per interaction charged to the existing
credits, a per-organization switch, and a guarantee of data parsimony: only schema and view are sent,
never the data. The transcript, the switch and cost metering are generic and asked of Mosaikit; the
parsimony of the context is how Geoportal writes its tools, and the panel states it so administrators
can verify it in the transcript.
