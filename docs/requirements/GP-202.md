A model has a history of revisions, and two revisions are compared by element identifier: what
appeared, what disappeared, what changed geometry or properties, as an inspectable list and as an
overlay of the two states. It reuses the comparison of two states of vector data (GP-167), which is the
same question asked of another kind of data. A design model changes every week: without revisions the
portal always shows a snapshot nobody can date.
