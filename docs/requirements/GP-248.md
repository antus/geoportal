A Content-Security-Policy (allowed sources for scripts, styles, images and connections, who may frame the product and what it may frame) and the other headers a minimum security baseline assumes: strict transport, no content-type sniffing, referrer policy, permissions policy, and the attributes of session cookies. Geoportal only has to declare the sources it adds, such as the frame of the map engine and the GeoServer endpoints.
