acceptance:
  - The conversion produces tilesets with compressed geometry and textures, smaller than the uncompressed output of the same model.
  - The layer offers quality presets (for example quality, balanced, fluid) instead of raw rendering numbers, and changing the preset changes the detail loaded.
  - The layer states the element count of the model and warns when it exceeds what the browser is expected to handle.
---

Geometry and texture compression at conversion, and on the map a preset between quality and fluidity
instead of the raw numbers of a performance tab, with an honest count of how many elements the browser
handles before it degrades. An MEP model of two million components does not open on trust: it opens
because someone decided what to leave out.
