A contribution to Geoportal should have tests like any other software. Mosaikit's @mosaikit/testing already starts a real installation for an extension; Geoportal adds the geospatial fixtures on top of it: a sample map, sample layers published in a test GeoServer workspace, sample datasets, and helpers to assert on what panels and widgets show after a selection or a filter.
