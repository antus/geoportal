A table of pages per organization (key, language, title, Markdown body, state, order, author and time), a public route that serves them, and editing next to branding and theme in the organization settings. The body is converted and sanitized on the server with an allow-list of elements, because the page that hosts user-written text is the same that hosts the sign-in form; no free HTML, not even for the platform administrator. It lets an organization keep its own texts (about, help, contacts) inside the product instead of in a fork.
