  - Map and dataset cards show last activity, owner and sharing state in the same positions as the dashboard, process, file and connection cards.

Cards of maps and datasets read with the same signals in the same places as the cards of the common extensions: thumbnail, presence, alert state, sharing and last activity. They use the card model of mosaikit-ext (EXT-060) rather than markup of their own.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 2. One card model with declared signals across four lists; presence as a snapshot in the list DTOs; fixed creation time shown as last activity and the owner missing from map cards.
- 13 October 2026: split — the cards of Geoportal resources stay here; presence is MK-033 and the sharing model is an MK need.
- 14 October 2026: split — the card model and the cards of dashboards, processes, files and connections are EXT-060 in mosaikit-ext; the cards of maps (`ext-maps`) and datasets (`ext-catalog-geodata`) stay here (ADR-0024).
