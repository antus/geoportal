The same product as a service in high availability: replicas of Geoportal and GeoServer, a managed
database, secrets outside the images, upgrades without downtime, and several organizations always on.
The `geoportal-cloud` Helm chart already wraps the Mosaikit chart with GeoServer; GeoServer replicas
need a shared configuration and the shared GeoFence database.
