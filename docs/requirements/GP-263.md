A service for citizens signs in with SPID or the electronic identity card (CIE), as the Italian digital administration code requires: the SAML2 profile of SPID with its metadata and assurance levels, CIE with its own, and the prescribed button and provider-choice sequence. A business decision comes first: accreditation as a service provider or going through an aggregator.
