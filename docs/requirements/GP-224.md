Pairs of elements that intersect between two disciplines, and the clash between a planned route and
what exists, as an inspectable list with position, overlap measure and snapshot. It is not a construction
clash-detection engine and must not be called one: it is the check needed to put a new network inside an
existing building, the GIS-BIM case this platform is the right place for.
