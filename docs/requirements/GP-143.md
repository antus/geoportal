An external penetration test on the boundaries Geoportal owns: isolation of organizations in GeoServer
(GeoFence rules, workspaces), the gateway that protects streams, databases, processes and OWS, AuthKey
access for desktop clients, share links of resources and the upload and ingestion path. Findings are
fixed and the test is repeated. The test of the platform itself (organization boundary of the kernel,
extension loader) is a generic need of Mosaikit.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: to do, wave 5. Scope was the multi-tenant boundary, share tokens, upload path and
  plugin loader.
- 13 October 2026: split — the platform part goes to MK-114; the geodata access layer
  stays in Geoportal. Estimate lowered from [10, 15] since the kernel part moved.
