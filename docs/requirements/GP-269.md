  - The export of an organization contains every dataset of its catalog as GeoPackage (vector) or COG (raster), with its metadata as RNDT/ISO 19139 XML.
  - Maps, dashboards and processes are in the export as documented JSON, and styles as SLD.
  - Importing the export into a second installation recreates an organization whose layers answer WMS GetMap from GeoServer and whose maps open with the same layers.
---

A public body buys on condition that it can leave: qualification rules ask for portability and tenders ask for an exit plan. The generic part (people, memberships, files, documents, exit procedure and certified deletion) is a platform need; Geoportal contributes its content in open formats: datasets as GeoPackage and COG, metadata as RNDT/ISO XML, styles as SLD, maps, dashboards and GeoFlow processes as documented JSON, in an archive a second installation can import.
