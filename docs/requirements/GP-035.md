The header always names the active organization and, with several memberships, lists them with the
role held in each. Switching asks about unsaved changes of the open resource and says what it closed;
a resource that does not belong to the active organization shows where it comes from.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 1. Required multiple memberships; switching verified the
  membership and a token for a revoked membership was refused.
- 13 October 2026: moved — Mosaikit covers memberships of several organizations and the organization
  selector (MK-017) and the active organization in personal settings (MK-027).
