Sensor networks on the map: stations filtered by state, readings against their own thresholds, trend and short forecast in one sparkline, detection of silent sensors. It is configured first for river levels, then reused for air quality, traffic and energy by changing thresholds and units. It runs on the common domain-plugin contract, not on a framework of its own.
