The shape decided in September 2026: categories on the left, configured sources in the middle with their pills, contents on the right with edits and shortcuts. The detail column does two jobs: detail when a source is chosen, form when "New" is pressed; and the connection test produces the preview, so creating and reading share one panel. The same structure, in two columns, is the "Add layer" dialog of the map, where the question is which piece to add. Connections created from a curated entry record their origin, so one entry is connected once and a change of its address reaches whoever connected it. In the new product the categories live in the list pane of the Mosaikit shell.
