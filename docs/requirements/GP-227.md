acceptance:
  - Elements carrying a life phase (existing, demolished, planned) can be shown or styled by phase.
  - With a construction sequence assigned to elements, moving the map time slider shows only the elements built by that date.
  - Playing the time slider animates the construction of the model.
---

Life phase (existing, demolished, planned) and construction sequence tied to the map time slider
(GP-164), so a model can be watched as a film rather than a photograph. It costs little because the time
slider exists; it is valuable in urban planning and on long building sites.
