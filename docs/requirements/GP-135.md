Two demonstrations that hold up to questions are worth more than six sketches. Two sectors, chosen
among smart city, transport, environment, agriculture, utilities and defence, each get a complete
chain: real source data, an ingestion process, a 3D map, a dashboard, an alert rule and a story. The
work needs domain knowledge more than engineering, and the chain must be duplicable into a customer
organization.
