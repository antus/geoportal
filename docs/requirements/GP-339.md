The legacy plan described the whole new interface from the navigable mockup: an icon sidebar with the
configuration entry at the bottom, search with results by type, the person's, appearance and help
menus, a three-step sign-in, the pages of the platform administrator (organizations, global people,
marketplace, monitoring, activity log, system), of the organization manager (people, modules, usage,
activity log, configuration) and of the user, plus the screens added by the mockup review. In the new
architecture the frame is Mosaikit's shell (MK-025, MK-027, MK-047) and most of those pages are
generic platform features. What stays Geoportal's is the launcher home of the Geoportal apps inside
the console, and the geodata screens: data rules with a preview of what a subject sees, and "why
can't this person see this data".

- 2026-10-15: the three criteria are implemented in `ext-catalog-geodata`. The home page of the console shows
  the widget **Le tue app** (`home.widget`, MK-101): a tile for each of catalog, maps, dashboards and processes
  that the app bar of the person shows (MK-030, MK-056) among the active extensions (MK-133). The dialog of a
  data rule previews what its subject will receive from WFS before it is saved (features out of all, columns,
  the first ten features), from the rule set the organization would have with it; the view **Perché non vede
  i dati** of the geodata app names, for a person and a layer, the missing grant or the data rules that hide the
  data. Both read GeoFence's decision from the rule set the catalog writes (`Evaluation`) and ask WFS with its
  limits (`Preview`); `AccessDiagnosisTest` writes the same rules into GeoServer and checks its WMS and WFS
  answers to the person. Tests tagged GP-339: `EvaluationTest`, `AccessDiagnosisTest` (GeoServer),
  `tests/console.test.ts`, `tests/console.spec.ts`. Every criterion is covered; it becomes done when MK-072,
  MK-058, MK-096 and MK-074, on which it depends, are done. The diagnosis is a view of the geodata app; moving
  it to a panel of the console (`console.panel`, MK-072) is left for when MK-072 is done.
