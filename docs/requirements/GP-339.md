The legacy plan described the whole new interface from the navigable mockup: an icon sidebar with the
configuration entry at the bottom, search with results by type, the person's, appearance and help
menus, a three-step sign-in, the pages of the platform administrator (organizations, global people,
marketplace, monitoring, activity log, system), of the organization manager (people, modules, usage,
activity log, configuration) and of the user, plus the screens added by the mockup review. In the new
architecture the frame is Mosaikit's shell (MK-025, MK-027, MK-047) and most of those pages are
generic platform features. What stays Geoportal's is the launcher home of the Geoportal apps inside
the console, and the geodata screens: data rules with a preview of what a subject sees, and "why
can't this person see this data".
