The Geoportal portable archive today has no GeoServer and no PostGIS. The full portable adds them:
PostGIS in the bundled PostgreSQL, GeoServer with GeoFence started with the database created at the
first start, free ports, start and stop in the right order, a check that everything answers, and
GeoServer listening only locally. Accounts stay in the product (local accounts, MK-003). The archive
carries the licence texts and the source offer of GeoServer and PostGIS, and a smoke test starts it
on a clean machine at every release.
