What each person finds after signing in should answer what happened while away and what waits for them. The user sees what they were working on, changes in the resources they follow and their alerts. The manager sees the state of the organization: usage against limits, members and pending invitations, what was shared outside, and failing processes. The administrator sees the health of the system and the queues that grow. These are three contents of one page model, not three pages; the identity already shown in the top bar is not repeated.
