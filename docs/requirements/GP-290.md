The sidebars of the list pages had a fixed width, and entries that did not fit wrapped to two lines. Each sidebar gets a drag handle, a width remembered per page, a double click to restore the default, and long names truncated with an ellipsis and the full text on hover.

- geoportal-legacy: done on 16 September 2026, wave 3.
- 13 October 2026: moved — the list pane of every app belongs to the Mosaikit shell (MK-025); resizing and remembering its width per app is a generic shell need.
