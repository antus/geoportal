---
id: GP-181
title: Late, out-of-order and duplicate observations are handled correctly
area: monitoring
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [GP-090, GP-084]
estimate: [4, 6]
legacy: R-181
acceptance:
  - Observations arriving hours late are stored at their own time and the resampled series is rewritten for that window only.
  - A late observation over threshold raises no alert as if it were now; if it raises one, the alert carries the true time.
  - The same message delivered twice by the broker is stored once.
---

A sensor silent for three hours empties its buffer all at once and the tail arrives with old times: ingestion must accept and reorder them, rewrite the resampled series only for the touched window, and not raise retroactive alerts as if they were current, or raise them with the true, declared time. Duplicates from an at-least-once broker must not count twice. It is the defect that does not show in a demo and shows in production the first night a repeater fails.
