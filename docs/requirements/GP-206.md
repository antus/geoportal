  - A six-plane section box with handles and a single section plane on any axis can be placed on a model.
  - The cut shows filled faces where walls and slabs are cut, not a hollow shell.
  - A section saved with the map is restored when the map is reopened and when its deep link is opened.
---

A six-plane section box with handles, a plane on any axis and above all a closed cut face instead of a
hollow shell: a cut model that shows the void inside its walls is the difference between a section and a
rendering error. The section is saved as part of the layer configuration, so it enters the map, the deep
link (GP-107), the thumbnail and the embedded panel without extra code.
