acceptance:
  - Turning on excavation for a model clips the terrain to the model footprint without drawing a polygon.
  - With transparent terrain, the underground parts of the model are visible from above ground.
  - In a sun and shadow study the model casts shadows on the terrain and on nearby buildings.
---

Excavation does itself: the terrain is clipped on the model footprint instead of on a hand-drawn polygon,
the underground is seen through transparent terrain, and the model becomes a body that casts shadows in
the sun studies of the urban planning tools (GP-093). The individual tools existed in the old viewer;
none of them knew that the building stood there.
