acceptance:
  - Public tiles and pages answer with a validator (ETag or Last-Modified) and a repeated request with it gets 304.
  - The sitemap lists exactly the public resources and public pages, and robots rules exclude everything else.
  - An address exceeding the anonymous rate limit gets 429 while signed-in people are still served.
---

The anonymous surface is the only part of the product anyone can call as often as they like: validators and cache headers on public pages and tiles (a validator, not a long expiry, a lesson already paid on plugin assets), a sitemap and robots rules generated from what is really public, and a rate limit per address. Without it, the first day a dataset lands on an aggregator is the first day the machine stops answering people with an account.
