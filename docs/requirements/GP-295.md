The data card follows the card of the other list pages: selection box, measures (how big, how fresh),
status and a footer with the people and the commands. It does not show map/dashboard/process badges on
a source: a database may contain both spatial and non-spatial tables, so that answer belongs to the
parts of the source, shown in the detail. The header has sorting and a grid/row switch, and its count
must match the cards shown. The unified list is the entry view of the page.
