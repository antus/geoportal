The home page answers four things: what this instance is, what the organization has, what changed recently, and one action. A visitor without an account sees the public face (public maps, dashboards, catalog); a member sees the organization's content. The home frame and its widgets are generic; Geoportal provides the public face and the widgets about its resources.
