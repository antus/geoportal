GeoFlow is the visual process editor, based on LiteGraph: a palette of nodes (fifty-two in the old
product) by category, rendered as cards with a coloured title bar, collapsible groups, automatic
layout, frames and annotations, links coloured by data type, and a guide panel per node with typed
inputs and outputs. It is the core of the processes extension of Geoportal.
