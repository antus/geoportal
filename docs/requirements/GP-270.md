  - Every release of Geoportal publishes a CycloneDX or SPDX bill of materials that lists the components of its extensions and of the images it ships (GeoServer, GeoLibre, PostGIS, pycsw).
  - The release build fails when the scan finds a known vulnerability above the declared severity threshold in any of those components.
  - The repository states the fix window per severity and the channel to report a security problem.
---

Secure-development guidelines ask for a bill of materials and a scan of known vulnerabilities, and a public body asks for them in writing the day a library makes the news. Mosaikit produces them for the kernel; Geoportal produces them for what it adds: its extensions and the third-party images of its distributions (GeoServer with its extensions, GeoLibre, PostGIS, pycsw), with a scan that breaks the release above a declared threshold and a declared fix window per severity.
