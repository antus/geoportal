area: bim

legacy: R-205
acceptance:
  - The storey selector lists the storeys of the model by name, with the elevations read from the IFC.
  - Choosing a storey hides everything above it, and the cut height matches the storey elevation declared in the model.
  - A plan view mode shows the chosen storey from above with an orthographic camera, and leaving it restores the previous camera.
---

One storey at a time, with elevations read from the model instead of dragged by hand, and the plan view
as a mode rather than a camera trick. A planar clip can already cut a model; here the cut knows that the
third floor exists and is called that.
