Three chains produce a layer to decide on: landslide susceptibility from morphology and change,
burnt area and active fires from spectral indices, flood extent from radar backscatter. Each is
released as a process, so it is schedulable and audited, and inherits history, repetition and quota.
We sell the products, not the models: what matters is the burnt-area map within an hour of the
satellite pass.
