Only one competitor publishes a price, so saying it where people look is a cheap argument. The
public page shows the entry price under the main buttons, read from the plans of the installation. A
cost comparison lets the visitor enter people, price per seat and horizon (one, three or five
years), with an optional detailed block. Three rules keep it honest: competitor prices are never in
the product (the visitor types them), our extras (storage beyond quota, premium connectors) are
counted too, and uncertain sums round against us.
