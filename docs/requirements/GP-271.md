Three files are the entry ticket to software reuse by public bodies: the licence, the publiccode.yml card required by the guidelines on acquisition and reuse of software to be listed in the national catalog, and the document that says how to report a vulnerability. They cost a day of writing, score points in public procurement, and are the minimum an open-source release presupposes.
