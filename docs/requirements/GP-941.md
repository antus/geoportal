Maps, dashboards, data, processes, people and connections belong to an organization with a name, a
unique domain, a plan and a state; a person sees only the resources of their organization, and the
system organization cannot be deleted.

- geoportal-legacy: done before v1.2.0, wave 0. Recorded for tracking on 25 September 2026.

  data of each organization, also for extension tables (MK-019).
