acceptance:
  - An alert of a rule whose policy names a person by mail reaches that person's mailbox and their activity feed, and not other members.
  - A rule whose policy names a webhook makes one signed POST to that address per alert transition.
  - A rule whose policy names a GeoFlow process starts a run of that process with the alert as input, visible in the process run list.
---

Delivery is a policy of each rule and each recipient, not a global setting: one rule warns the duty officer by SMS and the team in app, another only starts a process. Geoportal builds the delivery policy on its rules and the action "run a GeoFlow process" with the alert as input. The delivery channels themselves (in-app feed, mail, SMS, signed webhooks) are generic and come from Mosaikit.
