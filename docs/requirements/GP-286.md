Two questions underpin everything else. What does this source contain: the layers of a WMS, the feature types of a WFS, the tables of a database, the quantities of a sensor network, the collections of a STAC catalog, the processes of a WPS. Every source kind answers in the same shape: id, name, kind, geometry, capabilities. And what is it for: map, dashboard or process, derived from the kind and shown as pills. Capabilities belong to the piece, not to the source; the source shows only an estimate until its pieces are known. Contents are read by the server, which can reach services without CORS headers.
