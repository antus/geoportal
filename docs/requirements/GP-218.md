  - The attribute table opened on a model shows its elements with paging and sorting by any column.
  - Clicking a row zooms the 3D view to that element and selects it.
  - The table, with its current filter, exports to CSV and to a spreadsheet file.

The attribute table (GP-023) opened on the elements of a model: paging, sorting, zoom to the element
from the row, export to CSV and spreadsheet. It costs little once the table exists: the only thing
missing is the source, which is the element table (GP-201).
