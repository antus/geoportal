Avatars in the header of an open resource show the other people who have it open, excluding oneself,
with "+N" beyond four; two tabs of the same person count once. Access to the presence of a resource
requires the right to read it.

## History

- geoportal-legacy: done in v1.4.0, wave 1. A WebSocket room per open resource, with the token
  checked on opening against the read permission and the reason given on close.
- 13 October 2026: moved — presence on any resource is generic; MK-033 covers the presence of people
  and MK-031 the real-time channel, but not "who has this resource open", hence the MK need.
