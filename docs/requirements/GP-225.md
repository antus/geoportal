  - Importing a BCF file creates comments on the model, each with its camera viewpoint and referenced elements.
  - Opening an imported issue restores its camera and selects its elements.
  - Comments on a model export to a BCF file that a design tool can open with viewpoints and element references.
---

The exchange format for observations in the building trade (BCF: an issue with its viewpoint, camera and
elements), imported and exported on top of Geoportal's map comments (GP-040). It makes the portal a
counterpart of design tools rather than a dead end where an observation enters and never goes back.
Geoportal stays out of authoring: it receives, inspects and comments models, and BCF is how it talks to
those who draw them.
