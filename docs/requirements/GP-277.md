Synthetic thumbnails make two maps of the same sector look alike. At release time a capture opens
every sample map and dashboard in the viewer, waits for the scene to settle and stores a 16:9 image
as a file. Processes get a small diagram drawn from their real graph, coloured by node category. The
synthetic thumbnail remains as fallback. A coverage matrix states which feature appears in which
sample content and is checked when content is regenerated.
