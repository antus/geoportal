Sums of areas, volumes and counts grouped by property, as a table and as a dashboard panel, with
declared freshness and the revision they refer to. With a price list attached it becomes a cost, and
with revisions (GP-202) it becomes the difference in quantities between two revisions, which is the
figure argued over on a building site.
