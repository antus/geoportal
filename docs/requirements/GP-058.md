acceptance:
  - A layer above the size threshold is requested by the map as vector tiles (MVT or PMTiles), and no request downloads its full geometry set.
  - The map shows the same features at full zoom as the source data, with attributes available on click.
  - The map author does not choose or configure tiling; the layer is added like any other.
---

Large uploads are tiled on the server into vector tiles or PMTiles, and the map receives those instead of the raw geometries, transparently for the author. With data in PostGIS and GeoServer, vector tiles can come from GeoServer (with its tile cache) or from PMTiles produced at load time; the choice is part of the work.
