  - In the data grid a card has no image; it shows name, description, type with its logo, tags, and status and date in the footer.
  - The logo of a type is the same in the Type filter, on the card and on the first step of "New data".
  - When there are two filter columns they appear as two tabs, each using the full height of the side bar.
  - The selection box is visible on cards in both light and dark themes, and brand logos remain readable on dark backgrounds.

Small changes that lower the cost of reading a list of nearly two hundred sources. The card is compact
and flat (a WMS has no picture: it has a name, a type and a status); the type is shown by the real logo
of its connector, the same in the filter, on the card and in "New data", because all three ask the same
function. Status and date go in the footer. Filter columns become tabs when there are two. Entries with
zero results are not greyed out, and brand logos get a light tile so they stay visible in dark theme.
