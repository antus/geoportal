  - With "no data leaves" on for an organization, Geoportal features that call external services (hosted AI styling or analysis, external geocoders, external basemaps, external catalogs) show as off with the reason, and no request leaves for those hosts.
  - The residency page of the organization lists where its PostGIS data, GeoServer workspace, raster store, backups and logs live.
  - With the switch off, the same features work as before.
---

Critical data and services raise one question: where are the data and who can see them. The product must declare and enforce the boundary per organization: where the database, files, backups and logs live, and a switch saying "no data leaves this organization", on by default for organizations declared critical. The switch and the residency declaration are platform features; Geoportal declares where its own stores live (PostGIS schema, GeoServer workspace, COG store) and makes every feature that would send content outside (hosted AI, external geocoders and basemaps, external catalogs) show as off with the reason instead of trying.
