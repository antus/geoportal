Three roles (platform administrator, organization manager, user); what a user may do in each module
is given by a profile, extended by groups and personal exceptions. The permissions are declared in
the manifest of the module, and a new permission arrives switched off. The invitation chooses role,
profile and groups and previews the resulting permissions. The backend checks every request: hiding
a button is not enough. The legacy "collaborator" role becomes the profile "view and edit".

- 13 October 2026: moved — wholly generic. The three roles match Mosaikit's platform administrator,
  organization administrator and member, and groups are MK-032; permissions from manifests, profiles,

  their permissions in their manifests once it exists.
