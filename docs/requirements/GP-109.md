  - A map image block shows the map extent and layers chosen in the template.

A report definition is bound to the outputs of a process: a designed document with tables, charts
and map images, produced as PDF on demand or on the schedule that already runs the process, or at
the end of an execution or on a critical alert. Blocks of the template are bound to the named
outputs of the process.
