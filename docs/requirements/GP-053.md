Extends the data connections of Geoportal to cloud data warehouses: BigQuery and Snowflake first, Databricks next. They reuse what every connection has: encrypted credentials, connection test, health check and the query executor with a row limit. Geometry columns are read in the forms the warehouse exposes (native geography, WKB or WKT). Many organizations already keep their large tables in a warehouse and want to see them on a map without copying them.
