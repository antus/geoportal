  - With snapping on, a vertex dropped near a vertex or edge of a visible snappable layer lands exactly on it; with snapping off it does not.
  - Committing a self-intersecting polygon is refused with a message naming the feature and the problem, and nothing is written to the source.
  - A valid geometry is written unchanged, without silent repair.

Snapping to vertices and edges of the visible layers, which can be turned off, and validation of the
geometry at commit, with a reasoned refusal instead of a silent repair. The spatial index of visible
geometries used for snapping is the same one selection needs.
